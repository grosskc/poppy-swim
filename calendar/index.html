  1. 1

    Tap to subscribe

    On an iPhone or iPad, tap the button below. The Calendar app should open and ask you to subscribe. Then tap Subscribe. The calendar is named Poppy Swim Practice.

    Add to iPhone Calendar

    If the button does not open Calendar, use the steps in option 2.

  2. 2

    Or add it in Settings

    Open Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Add Subscribed Calendar. Paste this address:

    https://grosskc.github.io/poppy-swim/practices.ics

    Then tap Subscribe. Use this same https link on a Mac, or as a URL calendar in Google Calendar.

  3. 3

    Set your own alert, if you want one

    The feed does not include a default reminder, so each person can choose what works. After the calendar is added, open any practice in the Calendar app and add an alert, such as 30 minutes before.

The calendar asks phones to refresh about every 12 hours. Posted cancellations drop off after the next refresh. The weekly schedule also appears on the family dashboard.
